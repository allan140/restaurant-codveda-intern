About Us

AgAllan Restaurant was born from a simple belief:
great food brings people together. Located in the heart of Kampala, we blend authentic Ugandan flavors with continental finesse.
From the smoky aroma of our muchomo grill to the elegance of a perfectly seared steak, every plate tells a story of passion, quality, and home.

Our Vision

To serve delicious, high-quality meals and
Create lasting memories to our guests.

Our Values

Quality, integrity, customer satisfaction and
teamwork guide everything we do.

Our Promise

Fresh Ingredients, great taste and
warm, friendly hospitality every time.
